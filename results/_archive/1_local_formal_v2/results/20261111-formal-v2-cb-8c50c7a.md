## 1. Root Cause & Fix Summary (`commit 8c50c7a`)

In `1b54f37`, two continuous-batching (`CB`) late-join issues in `ditserve/core/src/control/cluster.cpp` slightly degraded bursty Gamma $CV=2.0$ performance (`91.0%` vs. `93.0%` for `abl-no-cb`):
1. **Non-leader late-join admission**: `try_cb_late_join` iterated over all GPU slots in a multi-GPU SP group (`sp_degree = 2`) without skipping non-leader ranks (`slot != leader_gpu`), allowing a second video request to attach to a follower GPU slot.
2. **Slack-gated late-join**: `try_cb_late_join` required `rem_leader_ms <= slack_ms` before admitting a waiting request into an active CB group, preventing requests that were waiting in queue from piggybacking on an already-running compatible batch when their remaining slack was smaller than a full single-request execution time.

Commit `8c50c7a` fixes both issues by enforcing `if (slot != leader_gpu) continue;` and matching active CB groups by compatible modality/shape and `active_reqs < max_cb_batch_size`.
